The story behind the work

About Me

“I believe data creates value when it helps people make better decisions.”

My career began in financial reporting, portfolio analysis and business performance, where I learned how evidence-based insights can help organizations identify challenges, improve operations and make informed decisions.

Today, as a Data Analytics and Business Intelligence Consultant at Davaya Corporation, I combine financial expertise, business understanding and technical analytics to solve real-world problems. My experience spans financial services, retail, customer operations and market research.

I use SQL, Python, Power BI, Tableau, Excel, SAS and predictive modelling to transform complex data into actionable insights, dashboards, performance reports and practical recommendations.

My continued professional development in data science, machine learning and applied AI reflects my commitment to delivering reliable, decision-ready analytical solutions.
